# Global Retail Banking Credit & Underwriting Policy Manual (2026)

## 1. Eligibility Criteria
- **Age Requirement:** Applicant must be between 21 and 65 years of age at the time of loan maturity.
- **Employment Status:** Minimum 12 months continuous employment with current employer, or 24 months continuous business operation for self-employed individuals.
- **Minimum Net Monthly Income:** $2,500 for Unsecured Personal Loans, $4,000 for Commercial / SME Loans.

## 2. Risk & Debt-to-Income (DTI) Guidelines
- **Standard DTI Cap:** The total Monthly Debt Obligations divided by Gross Monthly Income must not exceed **45%**.
- **Exception DTI Cap (45% - 50%):** Permissible only if Credit Score >= 750 AND Minimum Average Bank Balance >= 3 months of EMI.
- **Critical Risk (DTI > 50%):** Immediate Rejection without exception.

## 3. Credit Bureau & Repayment History Rules
- **Minimum FICO / Credit Bureau Score:**
  - Prime Tier (Tier 1): Score >= 750 (Qualifies for base interest rate: 6.5% - 8.0%)
  - Near-Prime Tier (Tier 2): Score 650 - 749 (Interest rate: 8.5% - 11.5%)
  - Subprime Tier (Tier 3): Score < 650 (Requires Co-Signer or 100% Cash Collateral)
- **Account Bounce / Insufficient Funds (NSF):**
  - Zero bounces in 90 days: Standard approval.
  - 1 bounce in 180 days: Warning flag; requires manual senior review.
  - 2 or more bounces in 180 days: Automatic rejection for high default probability.

## 4. Know Your Customer (KYC) & AML Screening
- **Identity Verification:** National ID / Passport must be valid, not expired, and tamper-free.
- **Sanctions & PEP (Politically Exposed Persons):** Mandatory automated screening against international sanctions lists (OFAC, UN, Interpol).
- **Address Verification:** Utility bill or official bank statement dated within the last 90 days.

## 5. Loan Limits & Tenure
- **Personal Unsecured Loan:** Maximum up to 5x Net Monthly Income, capped at $50,000. Tenure: 12 to 60 months.
- **Auto / Vehicle Loan:** Maximum up to 80% Loan-to-Value (LTV). Tenure: Up to 84 months.
